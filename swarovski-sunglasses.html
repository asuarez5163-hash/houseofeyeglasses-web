    About our Swarovski sunglasses

    69 sunglasses in stock, from $180 to $390. 28 models from Swarovski. Shape: cat-eye (28), oval (19), square (18) and geometric (4). For: women (69). Color: black (24), transparent (20), havana (7) and green (6). Lens widths run from 50 to 63 mm.

    Each product page lets you keep the original sun lenses or choose single-vision or progressive prescription lenses with a sun tint, made to your prescription. An optician checks every prescription before the lab starts.
