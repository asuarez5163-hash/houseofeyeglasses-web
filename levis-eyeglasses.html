    About our Levis frames

    We carry 64 Levis frames (48 models), every one new and in stock, from $46 to $50. Shape: round (27), rectangle (16), square (10), cat-eye (5), oval (4), browline (1) and geometric (1). Material: TR-90 (33), metal (18) and acetate (11). For: women (28), men (21) and unisex (15). Rim: full rim (63) and rimless (1). Lens widths run from 46 to 99 mm.

    Levis glasses with prescription lenses

    Any Levis frame on this page can be made with your prescription. Choose single-vision, progressive or computer lenses, clear or as prescription sunglasses, and add anti-glare, blue-light or light-responsive options. Stronger prescriptions can use thinner high-index lenses. You add your prescription in four short steps, and an optician checks every prescription.

    Standard U.S. shipping is free. Frames ordered without prescription lenses can be returned within 30 days.
