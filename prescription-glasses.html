    Prescription glasses online

    Choose any of our designer frames, give us your prescription, and we make the lenses for it: single vision, progressive or office. An optician in Tampa checks every order before the lab starts.

    Choose a frameCompare all lens options

    How ordering works

    Pick a frame and tap Select lenses. Four short steps follow: your ZIP code, how you will use the glasses, your prescription (type it or upload a photo) and the lens material and coatings.

    New to prescriptions? This guide explains every number and how to measure your PD at home. Find your frame size with the numbers on the glasses you own.

    Which lenses do I need?

    Single vision if your prescription has no ADD: one power, for far or for near. Progressive if it has an ADD and you want one pair for far, computer and reading, with no line. Office lenses for long hours at a desk.

    To any of them you can add a blue-light filter, light-responsive lenses or a sun tint.

    What it costs

    The lenses are added to the price of the frame you pick. You see the full price before you pay. Standard U.S. shipping is free. If we make an error filling your prescription, we remake the lenses at no cost (returns policy).

    What you need

    A current prescription from your eye doctor and your pupillary distance (PD). We cannot write or renew a prescription. In some states we also confirm it with your eye doctor’s office before making the lenses.

    Material and coatings

    Every pair starts with polycarbonate, a light, impact-resistant material that is included in the price. Stronger prescriptions can move to high-index lenses (1.67 or 1.74) to stay thin, and an anti-reflective coating takes the reflections off the lens.

      Questions

      Good to know

      Is it safe to order prescription glasses online?
      Yes, when the prescription is current and the measurements are right. An optician reviews every prescription we receive, and if we make an error filling it, we remake the lenses at no cost.
      Can I use the prescription I already have?
      Yes, as long as it has not expired. Type the numbers or upload a photo when you choose your lenses.
      How long does delivery take?
      Standard U.S. shipping is free and the estimated delivery time is 5–10 business days. Prescription lenses are made to order, so they need lab time before they ship. See the shipping policy.
      Can I return them?
      Frames bought without prescription lenses can be returned within 30 days, unworn. Prescription lenses are made for you, so they cannot be returned once the lab starts; if we made a mistake, we remake them free. Details in the returns policy.
