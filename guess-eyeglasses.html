    About our Guess frames

    We carry 456 Guess frames (242 models), every one new and in stock, from $37 to $60. Shape: rectangle (135), cat-eye (130), square (75), oval (46), aviator (28), geometric (21) and round (21). Material: TR-90 (174), acetate (147), metal (133) and mixed materials (1). For: women (320), men (117), unisex (16) and kids (3). Rim: full rim (440), half rim (10) and rimless (6). Lens widths run from 47 to 68 mm.

    Guess glasses with prescription lenses

    Any Guess frame on this page can be made with your prescription. Choose single-vision, progressive or computer lenses, clear or as prescription sunglasses, and add anti-glare, blue-light or light-responsive options. Stronger prescriptions can use thinner high-index lenses. You add your prescription in four short steps, and an optician checks every prescription.
