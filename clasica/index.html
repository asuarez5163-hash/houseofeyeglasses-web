        Which lenses do you need?

        A short guide to the lenses we make, so you can choose before you shop.

        Read the full lens guide
        Single vision
        One prescription across the whole lens, for seeing far or near.
        Progressive lenses
        Distance, middle and reading zones in one lens, with no visible line.
        Blue-light lenses
        A filter for long hours in front of phones and computers.
        Light-responsive lenses
        Clear indoors, they darken in sunlight, so one pair covers both.
        Anti-reflective coating
        Takes the shine off the lens: less glare from headlights and screens, and people see your eyes in photos.
