    Measure your PD

    PD (pupillary distance) is the space between the centers of your pupils, in millimeters.

    With a mirror

    1. Stand about 8 inches (20 cm) from a mirror, in good light.
    2. Close your right eye. Line up the ruler’s 0 with the center of your left pupil.
    3. Close your left eye, open your right, and read the mark at the center of your right pupil.
    4. Repeat three times and use the average.

    With a friend

    1. Sit face to face at the same height, about an arm’s length apart.
    2. Look past your friend at something at least 10 feet (3 m) away.
    3. Your friend lines up 0 with the center of one pupil and reads the mark at the other.
    4. Measure three times and use the average.
    50–75mm
    Typical adult rangeThe average adult measures about 63 mm. Kids measure less.
    63
    Single PDOne number: the whole distance from pupil to pupil.
    31.5 / 31.5
    Dual PDTwo numbers, one per eye, from the center of your nose to each pupil.

    Two numbers like 63/60 on a prescription usually mean distance and near PD, not right and left.

    Two numbers? Add them up

    If you measured each eye on its own, from the center of your nose, the sum is your single PD.
