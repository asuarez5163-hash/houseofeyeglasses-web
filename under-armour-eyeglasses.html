    About our Under Armour frames

    We carry 114 Under Armour frames (72 models), every one new and in stock, from $73 to $83. Shape: rectangle (79), square (11), round (10), oval (6), aviator (4) and cat-eye (4). Material: TR-90 (50), metal (33) and acetate (31). For: men (73), unisex (20), women (18) and kids (3). Rim: full rim (89), half rim (22) and rimless (3). Lens widths run from 50 to 99 mm.

    Under Armour glasses with prescription lenses

    Any Under Armour frame on this page can be made with your prescription. Choose single-vision, progressive or computer lenses, clear or as prescription sunglasses, and add anti-glare, blue-light or light-responsive options. Stronger prescriptions can use thinner high-index lenses. You add your prescription in four short steps, and an optician checks every prescription.

    Standard U.S. shipping is free. Frames ordered without prescription lenses can be returned within 30 days.
