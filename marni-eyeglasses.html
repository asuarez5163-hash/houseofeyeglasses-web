    About our Marni frames

    We carry 80 Marni frames (39 models), every one new and in stock, from $270 to $420. Shape: rectangle (33), oval (20), cat-eye (10), geometric (7), square (6) and aviator (4). Material: acetate (75), metal (4) and TR-90 (1). For: unisex (76), women (3) and men (1). Lens widths run from 51 to 62 mm.

    Marni glasses with prescription lenses

    Any Marni frame on this page can be made with your prescription. Choose single-vision, progressive or computer lenses, clear or as prescription sunglasses, and add anti-glare, blue-light or light-responsive options. Stronger prescriptions can use thinner high-index lenses. You add your prescription in four short steps, and an optician checks every prescription.
