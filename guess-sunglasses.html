    About our Guess sunglasses

    209 sunglasses in stock, from $46 to $60. 130 models from Guess. Shape: cat-eye (46), rectangle (46), square (43) and aviator (26). For: women (150), men (46) and unisex (13). Color: black (33), gold (26), blue (22) and pink (17). Lens widths run from 49 to 68 mm.

    How to order them

    Each product page lets you keep the original sun lenses or choose single-vision or progressive prescription lenses with a sun tint, made to your prescription. An optician checks every prescription before the lab starts.
