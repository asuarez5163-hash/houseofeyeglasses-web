    About our Miu Miu frames

    We carry 118 Miu Miu frames (65 models), every one new and in stock, from $377 to $696. Shape: rectangle (40), oval (32), square (18), round (9), cat-eye (8), aviator (5), geometric (5) and browline (1). Material: acetate (59) and metal (59). For: women (115), unisex (2) and men (1). Rim: full rim (97) and rimless (21). Lens widths run from 44 to 80 mm.

    Miu Miu glasses with prescription lenses

    Any Miu Miu frame on this page can be made with your prescription. Choose single-vision, progressive or computer lenses, clear or as prescription sunglasses, and add anti-glare, blue-light or light-responsive options. Stronger prescriptions can use thinner high-index lenses. You add your prescription in four short steps, and an optician checks every prescription.

    Standard U.S. shipping is free. Frames ordered without prescription lenses can be returned within 30 days.
