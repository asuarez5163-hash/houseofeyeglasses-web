    About our Just Cavalli sunglasses

    28 sunglasses in stock, all at $85. 17 models from Just Cavalli. Shape: square (24), round (3) and aviator (1). For: women (19) and unisex (9). Color: black (10), transparent (4), brown (3) and havana (3). Lens widths run from 50 to 61 mm.

    How to order them

    Each product page lets you keep the original sun lenses or choose single-vision or progressive prescription lenses with a sun tint, made to your prescription. An optician checks every prescription before the lab starts.

    Standard U.S. shipping is free. Sunglasses ordered without prescription lenses can be returned within 30 days, unworn and in their original packaging.
