    We carry 328 Saint Laurent frames (216 models), every one new and in stock, from $183 to $960. Shape: square (112), cat-eye (92), rectangle (44), aviator (27), round (25), oval (14), geometric (12) and browline (2). Material: acetate (267), metal (47), TR-90 (11) and mixed materials (3).     Saint Laurent glasses with prescription lenses

    Any Saint Laurent frame on this page can be made with your prescription. Choose single-vision, progressive or computer lenses, clear or as prescription sunglasses, and add anti-glare, blue-light or light-responsive options. Stronger prescriptions can use thinner high-index lenses. You add your prescription in four short steps, and an optician checks every prescription.
