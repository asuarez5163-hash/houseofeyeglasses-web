    About our DKNY sunglasses

    51 sunglasses in stock, from $46 to $63. 25 models from DKNY. Shape: cat-eye (18), square (11), oval (8) and rectangle (8). For: women (49) and unisex (2). Color: black (11), transparent (10), blue (5) and silver (5). Lens widths run from 47 to 59 mm.

    How to order them

    Each product page lets you keep the original sun lenses or choose single-vision or progressive prescription lenses with a sun tint, made to your prescription. An optician checks every prescription before the lab starts.
