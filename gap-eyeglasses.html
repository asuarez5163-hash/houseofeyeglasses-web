    About our GAP frames

    We carry 62 GAP frames (33 models), every one new and in stock, from $42 to $45. Shape: square (42), aviator (11), round (4), rectangle (3) and cat-eye (2). Material: acetate (47), metal (14) and TR-90 (1). For: men (22), women (22) and unisex (18). Lens widths run from 48 to 59 mm.

    GAP glasses with prescription lenses

    Any GAP frame on this page can be made with your prescription. Choose single-vision, progressive or computer lenses, clear or as prescription sunglasses, and add anti-glare, blue-light or light-responsive options. Stronger prescriptions can use thinner high-index lenses. You add your prescription in four short steps, and an optician checks every prescription.

    Standard U.S. shipping is free. Frames ordered without prescription lenses can be returned within 30 days.
