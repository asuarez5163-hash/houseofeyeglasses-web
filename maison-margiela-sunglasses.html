    About our Maison Margiela sunglasses

    28 sunglasses in stock, from $200 to $289. 12 models from Maison Margiela. Shape: square (15), rectangle (6), oval (4) and round (3). For: unisex (28). Color: black (12), beige (3), grey (3) and havana (3). Lens widths run from 51 to 69 mm.

    How to order them

    Each product page lets you keep the original sun lenses or choose single-vision or progressive prescription lenses with a sun tint, made to your prescription. An optician checks every prescription before the lab starts.
