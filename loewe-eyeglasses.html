    About our Loewe frames

    We carry 96 Loewe frames (61 models), every one new and in stock, from $218 to $340. Shape: square (33), cat-eye (22), rectangle (14), round (14), geometric (6), oval (4) and aviator (3). Material: acetate (86), metal (9) and mixed materials (1). For: women (87), men (5) and unisex (4). Lens widths run from 47 to 144 mm.

    Loewe glasses with prescription lenses

    Any Loewe frame on this page can be made with your prescription. Choose single-vision, progressive or computer lenses, clear or as prescription sunglasses, and add anti-glare, blue-light or light-responsive options. Stronger prescriptions can use thinner high-index lenses. You add your prescription in four short steps, and an optician checks every prescription.
