    36 sunglasses in stock, from $73 to $83. 27 models from Under Armour. Shape: rectangle (22), square (5), aviator (3) and oval (3). For: men (22), unisex (7), women (4) and kids (3). Color: black (15), green (4), havana (4) and transparent (4). Lens widths run from 53 to 72 mm.

    Each product page lets you keep the original sun lenses or choose single-vision or progressive prescription lenses with a sun tint, made to your prescription. An optician checks every prescription before the lab starts.
