    02 · Measure your face

    No glasses? Measure your face

    Your frames should be about as wide as your face at the temples. A standard card is all you need: credit, debit, ID and gift cards are all 85.6 mm (3⅜ in) long.

    1. 1Find your temples

      Face a mirror and look straight ahead. Your temples are the flat spots just outside the outer corners of your eyes.

    2. 2Card at the temple

      Hold the card level, just under your eyes, with one short edge at your left temple. Put a fingertip where the other edge ends.

    3. 3Slide it over once

      Move the card so it starts at your fingertip. See where your right temple falls along this second card.

    4Read your size

    Before the middle of the card: Narrow. From the middle to a little past it: Medium. Up to about two-thirds: Wide. Beyond two-thirds: Extra-wide.

    03 · Nose bridge

    Nose bridge fit

    Width is half of fit. The other half is where your frames rest on your nose.

    Choose low bridge if your glasses

    • slide down your nose all day
    • rest on your cheeks or leave marks on them
    • lift off your nose when you smile
    • let your lashes brush the lenses

    Coming with our collection A nose-bridge filter, standard or low, on every frame.

    04 · Face shape

    Frames for your face shape

    Pick the outline closest to yours. The rule of thumb is contrast: angles for soft faces, curves for strong ones.
