    About our Swarovski frames

    We carry 110 Swarovski frames (55 models), every one new and in stock, from $49 to $390. Shape: cat-eye (47), square (28), oval (20), geometric (10), round (3) and rectangle (2). Material: acetate (86), metal (23) and TR-90 (1). For: women. Rim: full rim (108) and rimless (2). Lens widths run from 50 to 63 mm.

    Swarovski glasses with prescription lenses

    Any Swarovski frame on this page can be made with your prescription. Choose single-vision, progressive or computer lenses, clear or as prescription sunglasses, and add anti-glare, blue-light or light-responsive options. Stronger prescriptions can use thinner high-index lenses. You add your prescription in four short steps, and an optician checks every prescription.

    Standard U.S. shipping is free. Frames ordered without prescription lenses can be returned within 30 days.
