    About our Adidas frames

    We carry 304 Adidas frames (154 models), every one new and in stock, from $58 to $88. Shape: rectangle (128), cat-eye (45), square (43), round (31), oval (22), aviator (21), geometric (11) and browline (3). Material: TR-90 (223), metal (44), acetate (19) and mixed materials (2). For: men (109), women (101) and unisex (94). Rim: full rim (262), half rim (23) and rimless (19). Lens widths run from 47 to 146 mm.

    Adidas glasses with prescription lenses

    Any Adidas frame on this page can be made with your prescription. Choose single-vision, progressive or computer lenses, clear or as prescription sunglasses, and add anti-glare, blue-light or light-responsive options. Stronger prescriptions can use thinner high-index lenses. You add your prescription in four short steps, and an optician checks every prescription.
