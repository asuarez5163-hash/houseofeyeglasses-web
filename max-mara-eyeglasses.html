    About our Max Mara frames

    We carry 300 Max Mara frames (149 models), every one new and in stock, from $58 to $85. Shape: cat-eye (112), square (53), oval (40), rectangle (34), round (34), geometric (21) and aviator (6). Material: acetate (167), metal (85), TR-90 (30) and mixed materials (17). For: women (299) and men (1). Rim: full rim (294) and half rim (6). Lens widths run from 50 to 68 mm.

    Max Mara glasses with prescription lenses

    Any Max Mara frame on this page can be made with your prescription. Choose single-vision, progressive or computer lenses, clear or as prescription sunglasses, and add anti-glare, blue-light or light-responsive options. Stronger prescriptions can use thinner high-index lenses. You add your prescription in four short steps, and an optician checks every prescription.

    Standard U.S. shipping is free. Frames ordered without prescription lenses can be returned within 30 days.
