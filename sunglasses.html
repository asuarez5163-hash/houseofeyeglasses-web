    Prescription sunglasses

    Turn any frame we carry into sunglasses: your prescription lenses with a solid or gradient sun tint, or light-responsive lenses that darken outdoors.

    Choose a frameCompare all lens options

    How it works

    Pick any frame, choose your lenses (single vision or progressive) and add Sun tint in the last step. The lab tints your prescription lenses, so you see as sharply in the sun as you do indoors.

    Sun tint or light-responsive?

    A sun tint is always dark: the choice for a dedicated pair of sunglasses, for driving and the beach. Light-responsive lenses are clear indoors and darken in sunlight, so one pair does both jobs; behind a windshield they darken less.

    What it costs

    The tint is added to the price of your prescription lenses and your frame. You see the full price before you pay. Standard U.S. shipping is free. If we make an error filling your prescription, we remake the lenses at no cost (returns policy).

    Good to know

    Polarized lenses and ready-made sunglasses are not in our catalog yet. Today the sun tint is made with prescription lenses, in polycarbonate or high-index.

      Good to know

      Can any frame be made into prescription sunglasses?
      Almost any frame we carry can take tinted prescription lenses. Larger frames give more coverage from the sun.
      Can I get progressive sunglasses?
      Yes. Choose progressive lenses and add the sun tint in the last step.
      Do you sell non-prescription sunglasses?
      Not yet. Today the sun tint is made with prescription lenses.
