    Prescription options

    Sunglasses, your way.

    Buy a sunglass as supplied, make it prescription, or turn an optical frame into a dedicated sun pair.

    Shop prescription-ready sunglassesChoose an optical frame to tint

    Wear them as supplied

    Choose any sunglass from the collection above and buy it with its original sun lenses. The product page shows the real frame, color, size and price from our inventory.

    Make them prescription

    On the sunglass product page, choose prescription lenses instead: single vision or progressive, made to your current prescription. An optician checks the order before the lab starts.

    Or tint an optical frame

    Prefer a frame from our optical collection? Choose any optical frame, select your prescription lenses and add a solid or gradient sun tint in the last step. Light-responsive lenses are the one-pair alternative.

    What it costs

    The original sunglass price appears on its product page. Prescription lenses and options are added to the frame price, and you see the full total before paying. Standard U.S. shipping is free.

    Questions

    Good to know

    Can I buy sunglasses without a prescription?
    Yes. Choose a sunglass from this page and keep its original sun lenses.
    Can I order prescription sunglasses?
    Yes. Open a sunglass, choose prescription lenses and enter or upload your current prescription. Single vision and progressive options are available.
    Can an optical frame become sunglasses?
    Yes. Choose an optical frame, select your prescription lenses and add a sun tint in the last step.
    Do you carry sunglasses for kids?
    Yes. Use the Kids filter above to see the children’s sunglasses currently in stock.
