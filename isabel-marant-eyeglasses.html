    About our Isabel Marant frames

    We carry 156 Isabel Marant frames (89 models), every one new and in stock, from $80 to $130. Shape: square (52), cat-eye (31), rectangle (27), geometric (18), aviator (15), oval (7) and round (6). Material: acetate (114), metal (38), mixed materials (2) and TR-90 (1). For: women (153) and unisex (3). Rim: full rim (151) and half rim (5). Lens widths run from 49 to 99 mm.

    Isabel Marant glasses with prescription lenses

    Any Isabel Marant frame on this page can be made with your prescription. Choose single-vision, progressive or computer lenses, clear or as prescription sunglasses, and add anti-glare, blue-light or light-responsive options. Stronger prescriptions can use thinner high-index lenses. You add your prescription in four short steps, and an optician checks every prescription.
