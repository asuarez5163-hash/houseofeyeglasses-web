    About our Off-White frames

    We carry 98 Off-White frames (63 models), every one new and in stock, from $185 to $350. Shape: square (46), rectangle (30), cat-eye (13), oval (5), geometric (3) and round (1). Material: acetate (89) and metal (9). For: unisex (62), women (35) and men (1). Rim: full rim (92), rimless (4) and half rim (2). Lens widths run from 49 to 141 mm.

    Off-White glasses with prescription lenses

    Any Off-White frame on this page can be made with your prescription. Choose single-vision, progressive or computer lenses, clear or as prescription sunglasses, and add anti-glare, blue-light or light-responsive options. Stronger prescriptions can use thinner high-index lenses. You add your prescription in four short steps, and an optician checks every prescription.

    Standard U.S. shipping is free. Frames ordered without prescription lenses can be returned within 30 days.
