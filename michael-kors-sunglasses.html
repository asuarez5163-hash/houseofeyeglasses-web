    65 sunglasses in stock, from $105 to $233. 35 models from Michael Kors. Shape: cat-eye (20), square (18), oval (11) and aviator (6). For: women (58), men (6) and unisex (1). Color: black (14), gold (11), tortoise (6) and pink (5). Lens widths run from 42 to 61 mm.

    Each product page lets you keep the original sun lenses or choose single-vision or progressive prescription lenses with a sun tint, made to your prescription. An optician checks every prescription before the lab starts.
