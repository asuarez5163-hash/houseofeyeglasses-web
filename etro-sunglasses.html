    About our Etro sunglasses

    39 sunglasses in stock, from $125 to $145. 25 models from Etro. Shape: cat-eye (15), square (13), oval (6) and rectangle (3). For: women (35), unisex (3) and men (1). Color: black (7), pink (6), brown (3) and gold (3). Lens widths run from 48 to 64 mm.

    How to order them

    Each product page lets you keep the original sun lenses or choose single-vision or progressive prescription lenses with a sun tint, made to your prescription. An optician checks every prescription before the lab starts.
