    About our Max & Co frames

    We carry 223 Max & Co frames (115 models), every one new and in stock, from $47 to $80. Shape: cat-eye (54), round (54), rectangle (38), square (37), oval (29), geometric (8) and aviator (3). Material: TR-90 (148), metal (52), acetate (15) and mixed materials (8). For: women. Rim: full rim (218) and rimless (5). Lens widths run from 15 to 131 mm.

    Max & Co glasses with prescription lenses

    Any Max & Co frame on this page can be made with your prescription. Choose single-vision, progressive or computer lenses, clear or as prescription sunglasses, and add anti-glare, blue-light or light-responsive options. Stronger prescriptions can use thinner high-index lenses. You add your prescription in four short steps, and an optician checks every prescription.

    Standard U.S. shipping is free. Frames ordered without prescription lenses can be returned within 30 days.
