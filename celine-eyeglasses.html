    About our Celine frames

    We carry 150 Celine frames (110 models), every one new and in stock, from $190 to $580. Shape: square (62), cat-eye (22), geometric (18), rectangle (18), oval (11), round (11), aviator (6) and browline (2). Material: acetate (139), metal (9) and TR-90 (2). For: women (106), men (28) and unisex (16). Rim: full rim (148) and half rim (2). Lens widths run from 48 to 69 mm.

    Celine glasses with prescription lenses

    Any Celine frame on this page can be made with your prescription. Choose single-vision, progressive or computer lenses, clear or as prescription sunglasses, and add anti-glare, blue-light or light-responsive options. Stronger prescriptions can use thinner high-index lenses. You add your prescription in four short steps, and an optician checks every prescription.

    Standard U.S. shipping is free. Frames ordered without prescription lenses can be returned within 30 days.
