    About our Adidas Original frames

    We carry 122 Adidas Original frames (72 models), every one new and in stock, from $73 to $75. Shape: square (64), rectangle (21), cat-eye (11), aviator (9), round (8), geometric (5) and oval (4). Material: acetate (67), TR-90 (35) and metal (20). For: unisex (56), men (41) and women (25). Rim: full rim (120), half rim (1) and rimless (1). Lens widths run from 47 to 64 mm.

    Adidas Original glasses with prescription lenses

    Any Adidas Original frame on this page can be made with your prescription. Choose single-vision, progressive or computer lenses, clear or as prescription sunglasses, and add anti-glare, blue-light or light-responsive options. Stronger prescriptions can use thinner high-index lenses. You add your prescription in four short steps, and an optician checks every prescription.
