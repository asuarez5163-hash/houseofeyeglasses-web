    About our Carolina Herrera sunglasses

    64 sunglasses in stock, from $110 to $130. 45 models from Carolina Herrera. Shape: square (30), cat-eye (13), oval (10) and rectangle (6). For: women (64). Color: black (20), havana (13), red (10) and pink (7). Lens widths run from 47 to 59 mm.

    How to order them

    Each product page lets you keep the original sun lenses or choose single-vision or progressive prescription lenses with a sun tint, made to your prescription. An optician checks every prescription before the lab starts.
