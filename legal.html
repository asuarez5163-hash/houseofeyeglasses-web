Legal & Policies

1. Privacy Policy

House of Eyeglasses LLC (“we,” “us,” or “the store”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit or shop with us online, and how we use it — including prescription information when you order prescription lenses.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone number.
  • Shipping and billing address.
  • Payment information (processed securely by our payment provider; we do not store your card numbers).
  • Prescription information you provide or upload (sphere, cylinder, axis, add power, pupillary distance, prescribing doctor’s name and contact information).
  • Order history and any communications with us.
  • Browsing data (cookies, IP address, device type) — see our Cookie Policy for details.

How We Use Your Prescription Information

  • To fill your order accurately and manufacture your prescription lenses.
  • To verify your prescription with your eye care provider when required or when something appears unclear, consistent with the FTC Eyeglass Rule.
  • We do not use prescription information for marketing purposes.

We are not a HIPAA-covered entity, but we treat prescription information as sensitive and limit access to staff who need it to fill your order.

Who We Share Information With

We do not sell or rent your personal or prescription information. We share it only with providers needed to run the store and fill your order — such as our e-commerce platform, our optical lab/manufacturing partner, shipping carriers, and, when necessary to verify a prescription, your prescribing doctor’s office.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal or prescription data (subject to recordkeeping requirements under Florida law), and you may opt out of marketing communications at any time. California residents: see our CCPA Privacy Notice for additional rights.

Children’s Privacy

Our store is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from minors without parental consent.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time and will post the date of the latest change here.

3. Cookie Policy

This site uses cookies and similar technologies to make the store work and to understand how it’s used. Prescription details you submit are stored securely on our servers and our e-commerce platform’s systems, not in cookies.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies: keep your cart and checkout working.
  • Functional cookies: remember preferences like language or currency.
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand traffic and site performance.
  • Marketing cookies: used only if you’ve enabled marketing tools such as retargeting ads (optional).

Managing Cookies

You can disable or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may prevent checkout from working correctly.

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third parties we work with, such as our payment processor and analytics providers, under their own privacy and cookie policies.

7. Returns Policy

Non-Prescription Frames

We accept returns of non-prescription frames that are unworn and in original packaging, within the return window published here before the store opens.

Prescription Lenses and Eyewear

  • Because prescription lenses are custom-made for you, they are not eligible for return or refund once fabrication has started, except as set out below.
  • If we made an error filling the prescription or details you submitted (wrong power, wrong PD entered by us, manufacturing defect), we will remake your lenses at no cost; contact us as soon as you notice the problem.
  • If your own submitted prescription or measurements were incorrect, a remake may be offered at a reduced fee — contact us to discuss options.
  • Frames purchased along with prescription lenses follow the same custom-order terms as the lenses once assembled.

How to Start a Return or Remake Request

Email us at amm@houseofeyeglasses.com with your order number, a description of the issue, and photos if applicable.

Return Shipping Costs

Return shipping for an error on our part is covered by us. For all other approved returns, return shipping is the customer’s responsibility.

Refunds

Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method; the time it takes to appear depends on your bank.
