    About our Skechers frames

    We carry 32 Skechers frames (22 models), every one new and in stock, from $41 to $43. Shape: rectangle (11), cat-eye (6), square (6), round (4), geometric (3) and oval (2). Material: acetate (14), TR-90 (12) and metal (6). For: women (19), men (7), kids (5) and unisex (1). Lens widths run from 45 to 54 mm.

    Any Skechers frame on this page can be made with your prescription.
