    About our Lacoste sunglasses

    62 sunglasses in stock, from $85 to $95. 36 models from Lacoste. Shape: square (38), rectangle (10), round (6) and aviator (4). For: men (44), unisex (9) and women (9). Color: transparent (11), black (10), blue (9) and havana (9). Lens widths run from 50 to 60 mm.

    How to order them

    Each product page lets you keep the original sun lenses or choose single-vision or progressive prescription lenses with a sun tint, made to your prescription. An optician checks every prescription before the lab starts.
