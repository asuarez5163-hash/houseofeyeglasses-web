    192 sunglasses in stock, from $213 to $960. 123 models from Saint Laurent. Shape: square (56), cat-eye (54), rectangle (30) and aviator (23). For: women (122), unisex (36) and men (34). Color: black (77), havana (43), transparent (20) and gold (11). Lens widths run from 46 to 75 mm.

    How to order them

    Each product page lets you keep the original sun lenses or choose single-vision or progressive prescription lenses with a sun tint, made to your prescription. An optician checks every prescription before the lab starts.
