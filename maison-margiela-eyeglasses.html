    About our Maison Margiela frames

    We carry 52 Maison Margiela frames (18 models), every one new and in stock, from $200 to $289. Shape: square (35), rectangle (10), oval (4) and round (3). Material: acetate. For: unisex. Lens widths run from 51 to 69 mm.

    Maison Margiela glasses with prescription lenses

    Any Maison Margiela frame on this page can be made with your prescription. Choose single-vision, progressive or computer lenses, clear or as prescription sunglasses, and add anti-glare, blue-light or light-responsive options. Stronger prescriptions can use thinner high-index lenses. You add your prescription in four short steps, and an optician checks every prescription.

    Standard U.S. shipping is free. Frames ordered without prescription lenses can be returned within 30 days.
