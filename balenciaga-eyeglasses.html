    About our Balenciaga frames

    We carry 45 Balenciaga frames (30 models), every one new and in stock, from $258 to $565. Shape: cat-eye (20), rectangle (13), geometric (5), oval (4), square (2) and aviator (1). Material: acetate (29), metal (11) and TR-90 (5). For: women (26), unisex (10) and men (9). Rim: full rim (38), half rim (6) and rimless (1). Lens widths run from 51 to 99 mm.

    Balenciaga glasses with prescription lenses

    Any Balenciaga frame on this page can be made with your prescription. Choose single-vision, progressive or computer lenses, clear or as prescription sunglasses, and add anti-glare, blue-light or light-responsive options. Stronger prescriptions can use thinner high-index lenses. You add your prescription in four short steps, and an optician checks every prescription.
