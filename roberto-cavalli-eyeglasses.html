    About our Roberto Cavalli frames

    We carry 76 Roberto Cavalli frames (45 models), every one new and in stock, from $180 to $280. Shape: cat-eye (24), square (20), rectangle (12), round (10), aviator (8) and oval (2). Material: acetate (61) and metal (15). For: women (66) and unisex (10). Rim: full rim (74) and rimless (2). Lens widths run from 49 to 99 mm.

    Roberto Cavalli glasses with prescription lenses

    Any Roberto Cavalli frame on this page can be made with your prescription. Choose single-vision, progressive or computer lenses, clear or as prescription sunglasses, and add anti-glare, blue-light or light-responsive options.
