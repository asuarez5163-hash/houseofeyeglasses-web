    About our Quay frames

    We carry 27 Quay frames (9 models), every one new and in stock, all at $58. Shape: square (12), cat-eye (6), aviator (4), rectangle (3) and round (2). Material: acetate (15), metal (7) and TR-90 (5). For: women (20) and unisex (7). Lens widths run from 36 to 60 mm.

    Quay glasses with prescription lenses

    Any Quay frame on this page can be made with your prescription. Choose single-vision, progressive or computer lenses, clear or as prescription sunglasses, and add anti-glare, blue-light or light-responsive options. Stronger prescriptions can use thinner high-index lenses. You add your prescription in four short steps, and an optician checks every prescription.
