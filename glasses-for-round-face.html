    Face shape

    Glasses for a round face

    A round face is about as wide as it is long, with soft curves and full cheeks. Frames with straight lines and corners give it definition.

    Choose a frameNot sure of your face shape? Take the style quiz

    How to tell if your face is round

    Your cheekbones are the widest part of your face, your forehead and jaw are about the same width, and your jawline is rounded rather than angular. Face length and width are close to equal.

    Frames that suit a round face

    Angles balance curves: rectangle, square, geometric and browline frames add structure, make the face look a little longer and draw a clearer line across the cheeks. Frames that are wider than they are tall work best.

    What to skip

    Small, perfectly round frames repeat the shape of the face and make it look fuller. If you love round glasses, pick a larger pair with a strong bridge or a keyhole.

    Size and fit

    Whatever the shape, the frame should be about as wide as the widest part of your face, and your eyes should sit near the center of each lens. Find your frame size with the numbers printed on the glasses you own.

      Questions

      Good to know

      Can a round face wear round glasses?
      Yes, with some care: go a little oversized, choose a thicker rim and avoid tiny lenses. Angular shapes are simply the easier choice.
