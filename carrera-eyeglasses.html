    About our Carrera frames

    We carry 315 Carrera frames (187 models), every one new and in stock, from $49 to $103. Shape: rectangle (145), square (83), aviator (32), round (19), oval (18), geometric (12) and cat-eye (6). Material: TR-90 (111), metal (102), acetate (100) and mixed materials (2). For: men (221), women (49), unisex (35) and kids (10). Rim: full rim (283), half rim (30) and rimless (2). Lens widths run from 46 to 99 mm.

    Carrera glasses with prescription lenses

    Any Carrera frame on this page can be made with your prescription. Choose single-vision, progressive or computer lenses, clear or as prescription sunglasses, and add anti-glare, blue-light or light-responsive options. Stronger prescriptions can use thinner high-index lenses. You add your prescription in four short steps, and an optician checks every prescription.

    Standard U.S. shipping is free. Frames ordered without prescription lenses can be returned within 30 days.
